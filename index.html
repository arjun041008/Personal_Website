Hello, I'm Arjun

A student tinkering with machine learning, mathematical and computational modelling, and plenty of adjacent—and not-so-adjacent—fields, in the hope of having an impact.

I've been fascinated by quite a few things over the years. It started with animals, then moved on to geography, then geopolitics, international relations, and history—and lately it has settled on machine learning and mathematical and computational modelling, and, slowly, on understanding how these models actually work.

If there's a connecting thread, it's the idea of trying to predict the future. I find that genuinely interesting—in part because I think it's the best way we can prepare for what's coming. That pull is what drew me to scientific research, and it's something I hope to keep pursuing.

Beyond the academic side, I'm a food aficionado and an equestrian. I'm always on the lookout for new opportunities, and I'm happy to talk about anything I've worked on that you might find interesting.
